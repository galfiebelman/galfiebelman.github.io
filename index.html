Gal Fiebelman

I am a Computer Science PhD Candidate at The Hebrew University of Jerusalem, under the supervision of Dr. Sagie Benaim. My research focuses on generating, editing and animating 3D and 4D content, combining diffusion models with 3D structure and physics. I am a recipient of the VATAT Fellowship for Outstanding PhD Students in AI and Data Science, and currently an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon Prime Video Sports.

I received my MSc from Tel Aviv University in Computer Science under the supervision of Dr. Hadar Averbuch-Elor and my BSc from The Hebrew University of Jerusalem in Mathematics and Computer Science. I also spent a few years in the industry developing Computer Vision algorithms.
